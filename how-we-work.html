How it works

Simple process. Practical output.

1. Connect

Your spreadsheets, and data from your other systems (payroll, CRM, rostering, accounting).

2. Shape

Clean the data and agree the definitions: what counts as a sale, a cancellation or an on-time delivery, so every number means the same thing to everyone.

3. Publish

Dashboards, automated updates and reporting packs designed to be read and acted on.

What you'll need to do

About an hour of your time for a kickoff call, and read-only access to your spreadsheets and systems. Nothing to install and no new systems to learn.

The question everyone asks

“Can I have it in Excel?” Yes.

In the data world that question usually gets an eye-roll. Not here. I'm technology agnostic: if your team lives in Excel, the report lands in Excel. The tool should fit the business, not the other way around.

  • Excel, done properly. A workbook that updates itself from the source, with the same numbers as any dashboard, so nobody copies and pastes at month-end.
  • Use what you've already got. Already paying for Power BI, Salesforce or your ERP's reporting? I build on it rather than asking you to replace it.
  • New tools only if they earn it. If a new licence would genuinely save you time or money, I'll show you why. If it wouldn't, you won't hear about it.

Why clarity matters

Three questions you should be able to answer on the 3rd.

Sales

Which customers are cancelling, and why?

Cancellations and churn usually surface weeks later, buried in the month-end figures. The Sales report shows them by customer, product and reason as they happen, alongside what's selling and what's in the pipeline.

Purchasing

Would you spot a 15% jump in supplier or freight costs before it hit your margin?

Fuel, freight and supplier price rises flow quietly into your margin. The Purchasing report tracks cost movement and late suppliers, so you see it in the week it happens.

Payroll & overtime

Is overtime growing, and can you explain why?

Overtime creeps up a few hours at a time. The Payroll & overtime report shows where it's growing and, where the data allows, links hours to output.
