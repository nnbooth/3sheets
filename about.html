About

An accountant who also builds the dashboards.

Dashboard builders often don't understand the numbers, and accountants often can't build the dashboards. I do both. Most of my 20 years has been spent in sales and operational reporting, tying non-financial data back to financial performance to work out what's really driving revenue and cost.

20 years, across the business

I've worked in mining and civil construction, property, engineering, insurance, healthcare diagnostics, manufacturing, auctions and legal services, for NYSE-listed and ASX-listed companies and for SMEs.

Part-time, not full-time

Most SMEs need what a management accountant brings, but not 40 hours a week of it. I work a piece at a time: a costing review this quarter, a forecast next, ongoing reporting if it helps, and you swap pieces in and out as the business changes.
