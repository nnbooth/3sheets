About

Problems solved

The gaps that keep you waiting for month-end.

Unclear sales performance

A consolidated view of revenue trends, cancellations, churn reasons, rep activity and pipeline movement, so you can see what's coming in and where performance is shifting.

Slow or unreliable purchasing visibility

Track purchase orders, supplier delays, overdue items, cost changes and delivery reliability, so it's easier to see where timelines or costs are moving.

Rising labour costs and overtime

Identify overtime patterns, labour cost changes, team imbalance and staffing pressure points. Where the data allows, link labour effort to output for clearer context.

Why clarity matters

Three questions you should be able to answer on the 3rd.

Sales

Which customers are cancelling, and why?

Cancellations and churn usually surface weeks later, buried in the month-end figures. The Sales report shows them as they happen, alongside what's selling and what's in the pipeline.

Purchasing

Would you spot a 15% jump in supplier or freight costs before it hit your margin?

Fuel, freight and supplier price rises flow quietly into your margin. The Purchasing report tracks cost movement and late suppliers, so you see it in the week it happens.

Payroll & overtime

Is overtime growing, and can you explain why?

Overtime creeps up a few hours at a time. The Payroll & overtime report shows where it's growing and, where the data allows, links hours to output.
