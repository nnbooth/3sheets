About

A CPA who also builds the dashboards.

Dashboard builders often don't understand the numbers, and accountants often can't build the dashboards. I do both. Most of my 20 years has been spent in sales and operational reporting, tying non-financial data back to financial performance to work out what's really driving revenue and cost.

20 years, across the business

I'm a CPA who has worked in mining and civil construction, property, engineering, insurance, healthcare diagnostics, manufacturing, auctions and legal services, for NYSE-listed and ASX-listed companies and for small businesses.

Sales and operational reporting

I've built reporting across sales, customer service, technical support, HR, payroll, time and attendance, and inventory, including a Power BI platform used across Australia, New Zealand and the Pacific Islands. Getting the definitions right (what counts, what's excluded, FX, territory changes) matters as much as the chart.

Part-time, not full-time

Most small businesses need what a management accountant brings, but not 40 hours a week of it. I work a piece at a time: a costing review this quarter, a forecast next, ongoing reporting if it helps, and you swap pieces in and out as the business changes.

Why clarity matters

Three questions you should be able to answer on the 3rd.

Sales

Which customers are cancelling, and why?

Cancellations and churn usually surface weeks later, buried in the month-end figures. The Sales report shows them by customer, product and reason as they happen, alongside what's selling and what's in the pipeline.

Purchasing

Would you spot a 15% jump in supplier or freight costs before it hit your margin?

Fuel, freight and supplier price rises flow quietly into your margin. The Purchasing report tracks cost movement and late suppliers, so you see it in the week it happens.

Payroll & overtime

Is overtime growing, and can you explain why?

Overtime creeps up a few hours at a time. The Payroll & overtime report shows where it's growing and, where the data allows, links hours to output.
